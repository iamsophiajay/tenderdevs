FAQ

Questions parents usually ask

What are coding classes for kids?+
Structured programmes that teach children programming fundamentals through interactive lessons, games, and projects tailored to their age group. At TenderDevs, we make it hands-on, fun, and always connected to real outcomes your child can be proud of.
How do you explain coding to a child?+
We use simple analogies — like recipes or step-by-step instructions — and start with visual, block-based coding (like Scratch) before introducing text-based languages. Children see their code create something real and fun, which builds both understanding and confidence quickly.
What is a good age to start coding?+
Around age 7 is ideal for structured coding classes, though children can begin earlier with visual tools like Scratch Jr. Our Foundation track is specifically designed for ages 7–10, with activities calibrated for their attention span and cognitive development.
Is coding easy to learn?+
Like any skill, coding takes practice — but with the right teaching approach, children find it enjoyable and progress faster than most parents expect. Our instructors are trained to make complex ideas accessible and to celebrate every small win along the way.
Are classes fully online? What platform do you use?+
Yes — all TenderDevs classes are delivered live online via Google Meet. Your child just needs a device and internet connection. Classes are interactive, small-group sessions so every student gets real attention from the instructor.
How do I know my child is making progress?+
We provide regular progress reports for parents, covering what your child has learned, projects completed, and next steps. You'll always be in the loop — and your child's projects will speak for themselves!
